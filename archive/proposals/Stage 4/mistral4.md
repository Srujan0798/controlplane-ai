### **Slide 2 — The Decision System**

- **Exact Headline**:  
  Blast Radius × Verdict Severity

- **Primary Visual**:  
  **4×4 matrix** (dominant, 75% of slide):  
  - **Rows**: R0 (top) → R1 → R2 → R3 (bottom)  
  - **Columns**: Low → Medium → High → Critical (left to right)  
  - **Cells**: Color-coded **PASS** (green), **ESCALATE** (yellow), **BLOCK** (red). Example: Entire R3 row = BLOCK (red).

- **Supporting elements**:  
  - Hard gate on actions  
  - Text streams optimistically with short hold-back

- **Design notes**:  
  Matrix is the sole focus. Headline above, supporting text in small font below. Use **subtle grid lines** and **bold color fills** for cells.
