### Beat-by-Beat Script

**Beat 1 — The Failure**  
*0:00–0:30*

**What is on screen:**  
Black background. White monospace text appears: `REFUND APPROVED: ₹1,84,000. Clause 7.2.` A green `PROCESSED` stamp slams over it. Below, a second line fades in: `AUDIT: Clause 7.2 not found in policy document.` At 0:20, the text blurs and resolves into the left side of Slide 1 (ghosted, grayed-out “TEXT SCORING” with a quality-score badge). The right side of Slide 1 begins to glow.

**Spoken words (exact):**  
"Last Tuesday, an AI agent approved a one lakh eighty-four thousand rupee refund. Cited clause seven point two. The clause does not exist in the policy document. The money moved. The customer was notified. Six hours later, a human found the error. The dashboard was green. The logs were complete. The system didn't fail. It was never asked to prove anything."

**Delivery notes:**  
Flat, documentary pace. Pause for one full second after "moved." Pause for one full second after "complete." The final sentence is slower, with a hard stop after "anything."

---

**Beat 2 — The Reframe**  
*0:30–1:15*

**What is on screen:**  
Slide 1 right side dominates. The refund paragraph decomposes into three CLAIM nodes. Tethers animate down to the PROVENANCE bar: `source | ACL | hash`. The first two claims flash green (`SUPPORTED`). The third flashes red (`UNSUPPORTED`). At 1:08, tight shot on the red claim node pulsing.

**Spoken words (exact):**  
"Every other tool scores text after it is generated. We do not score. We bind. At context assembly, before the model ever responds, we capture provenance. Source. ACL. Hash. Outside the model. The response enters as a set of claims requesting permission to act. Default state: unsupported. Now nothing acts until it can prove it should."

**Delivery notes:**  
"We do not score. We bind." — punch each word, 0.5-second pause between the two sentences. "Source. ACL. Hash." — staccato, one-second pause after each. The line "Now nothing acts until it can prove it should" is delivered as a single, quiet statement. No uplift.

---

**Beat 3 — The Graph**  
*1:15–2:15*

**What is on screen:**  
Black background. The graph chain appears in white monospace: `STEP → SPAN → CLAIM → ACTION`. The refund trace enters as a moving pulse. Three sequential reads animate without cutting away from the graph:

- **1:20–1:35:** Performance read. The CLAIM node `Clause 7.2 authorizes ₹1,84,000` tethers to the PROVENANCE bar; the bar shows no match; the claim turns red.
- **1:35–1:50:** Cost read. The same graph re-highlights. The walk backward animates: `ACTION ← CLAIM ← SPAN ← STEP`. Dead compute accumulates in red numerals beside each node.
- **1:50–2:05:** Responsibility read. The ACL column in the PROVENANCE bar highlights; the claim flashes orange for entitlement violation.
- **2:05–2:15:** All three reads ghost back to the unified graph. Text appears below: `Not three tools. One control plane.`

**Spoken words (exact):**  
"One graph. Three reads. Step. Span. Claim. Action. The graph is built at context assembly. Source, ACL, hash. We do not trust the model to remember what it saw. The performance read asks: is this claim supported by the evidence graph? Clause seven point two is not in the graph. Unsupported. The cost read asks: what dead compute does this response create when we walk it backward? Rework. Reprocessing. Human reversal. The responsibility read asks: does this claim hold the ACL for the data it touched? It does not. All three are reads on the same structure. Not three tools. One control plane."

**Delivery notes:**  
Steady systems rhythm. Do not accelerate. Let the visual animation lead; the voice follows it. "Unsupported" is a single flat word, not a warning. "One control plane" is the end of the beat—no trailing breath.

**Beat 4 — The Decision**  
*2:15–2:45*

**What is on screen:**  
Slide 2 matrix wipes in at 2:16, full screen, for exactly 4 seconds. The `R3 × UNSUPPORTED` cell is solid red with white text: `ESCALATE`. An annotation arrow points to it: `₹1,84,000 / clause 7.2`. At 2:20, the matrix wipes to a split screen. Left: `ACTION` with a geometric hard-gate icon. Right: `TEXT` with tokens streaming through a short hold buffer. At 2:35, the split fades to Slide 3 right column: `Per-route false-negative rate. Published. Auditable.`

**Spoken words (exact):**  
"An unsupported claim does not default to block. We ask: what is the blast radius? A categorical error at R3 escalates with a structured evidence packet. Actions face a hard gate. Text streams optimistically, with short hold-back. We publish our own per-route false-negative rate. We tell you exactly what we miss."

**Delivery notes:**  
Matrix on screen is silent except for the words "blast radius" and "R3 escalates." The split-screen section is faster, declarative. The final two sentences are slower, almost confessional. Emphasize "exactly."

**Beat 5 — The Land**  
*2:45–3:00*

**What is on screen:**  
Fade to black at 2:45. At 2:47, white text appears: `It used to be a bad paragraph.` At 2:53, a second line appears below it: `It is now an executed transaction.` The text holds, static, until the hard cut at 3:00.

**Spoken words (exact):**  
"It used to be a bad paragraph. It is now an executed transaction."

**Delivery notes:**  
First sentence: slow, almost nostalgic. Second sentence: same pace, no uplift, finality. After "transaction," absolute silence. No breath, no music swell.

### Closing Discipline

**Exact final spoken line:**  
"It is now an executed transaction."

**What happens visually in the last 5–8 seconds:**  
Both lines of the closing text hold static on a black screen. No motion. No logo. No fade-out. The screen cuts to black at exactly 3:00.

**What is deliberately left unsaid:**  
No "thank you." No "imagine the possibilities." No contact information, no website, no social handle, no call to action. The transaction is the end.

### Rules Applied

1. **One trace, end-to-end.** The ₹1,84,000 refund / clause 7.2 is the only scenario shown. No cycling, no hypotheticals.
2. **Vocabulary ban.** No "monitor," "detect," "observe," "guard," or "trust score." The script uses only authorize, prove, bind, refuse, hold, escalate, and block.
3. **No LLM-as-judge framing.** The system is never described as a model checking another model. Verification grounds out in captured provenance, ACL, and hash.
4. **Matrix brevity.** The Blast Radius matrix appears on screen for exactly 4 seconds. There is no grid lecture.
5. **Hard stop rule.** The final 15 seconds contain exactly the two-sentence closing line, then silence. No trailing sentences, no soft landing.
6. **Systems-clean language.** No "AI safety," "responsible AI," "ethics," "trust," or "human in the loop." The narrative is built entirely on execution, entitlement, and evidence.
