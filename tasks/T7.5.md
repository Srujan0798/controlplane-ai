  0:20-0:40 THE REFRAME. STEP → SPAN → CLAIM → ACTION. 'Not text to be scored — claims requesting permission to act.'

  2:30-3:10 THE NUMBERS. make eval → FNR with its interval. Dead compute. 'Every team will claim detection. We publish what we miss.'

PRODUCTION: rehearse the command sequence until it runs clean — a fumble cannot be edited around. Large terminal font. Nothing personal in frame, no notifications, no home paths. Record voiceover separately so a stumble costs one take.
