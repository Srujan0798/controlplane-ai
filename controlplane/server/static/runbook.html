60-second judge script

What to say while clicking. Every line names a claim, a graph, an action or a measurement. One trace — the ₹1,84,000 refund — start to close. Do not cycle three shallow scenarios.

Open bay and Admit 1:00
0–8s

Hook — stand on Clearance, refund + enforce already selected

Say: “A refund agent emitted: Approved. Refund of ₹1,84,000 issued under clause 7.2. Confidence point-nine-four. Money moved Tuesday, found Friday. Clause 7.2 does not exist. The company wrongly paid. The system didn't fail. It was never asked to prove anything.”

Click: nothing yet. Let the sentence land.

Do not say: the customer lost money. Do not say the refund was denied. Do not say clause 7.2 caps or doesn't cover.

8–18s

Admit — orange key

Say: “We do not score the paragraph. We check whether the claim is a member of the evidence the model was actually given. Everyone watches the exit. Nobody records the entrance.”

Click: Admit. Watch STEP → SPAN → CLAIM → ACTION lamps, then the strips cascade.

Do not say: we detect hallucinations. Do not say we read the model's mind.

18–35s

Dual action — point at the two flaps

Say: “Two pending actions on one ledger. Show text is R1 × entitlement — Edit, stripped. Issue refund is R3 × unsupported-categorical — Escalate, held with the evidence packet. Both correct at once. Proof scales with consequence.”

Click: the two strips. Optionally open Evidence receipts.

Do not say “blocked” about the refund. Say held and escalated with the packet.

35–48s

Mechanism — clause absence, not conflict

Say: “Clause 7.2 does not exist — absence of evidence, not conflicting evidence. That is why this cell is Escalate, not Block. Default verdict is UNSUPPORTED. A claim must earn SUPPORTED. Not low confidence. Unproven.”

Click: Matrix, already on R3 × Unsupported + categorical. Or stay on receipts and name clause_72 spans=(none).

Do not redraw the matrix. Do not invent STREAM / Kill Span / Terminate Step.

48–60s

Honesty — FNR empty on purpose

Say: “We publish our own miss rate. Per route. Not what we caught — what we missed. Right now the schema is empty. That emptiness is the credibility play. Lane 1 is deterministic. Latency target is ≤40 milliseconds p50 and ≤200 milliseconds p95 on R0/R1. Now nothing acts until it can prove it should.”

Click: Metrics cockpit, then download audit.jsonl from the overlay or Audit.

Do not quote 40ms as p95. Do not invent an FNR percentage. Do not drop bias if asked — counterfactual flip rate, route-level, async.
